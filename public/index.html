Simple Procedure

How To Complete Your Payments

Follow these four simple steps to successfully execute payments and clear outstanding balances.

1

Access Portal

Register a student account or log in to your existing account using your matric/registered email address.

2

Select Fee Category

Navigate to your fees panel, review your department's outstanding invoices, and choose the fee you want to pay.

3

Process on OPay

Enter the amount, redirect securely to the OPay checkout environment, and pay via Transfer, Card, or Wallet.

4

Print Official Receipt

Upon verification, retrieve your instant, computer-generated receipt from your portal transaction history.
